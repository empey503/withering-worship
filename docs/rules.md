# Withering Worship — Rulebook

**A Deck Building Board Game — 1–6 Players**

Centuries after civilization collapsed, humanity has returned to scattered tribes, crude settlements, forgotten ruins, and ancient faith.

From the ashes emerged **Seven Gods**, each representing a different force within the broken world. Six of these gods are worshipped by humanity. Their priests, shamans, witches, and war-mages wield genuine divine power, proof that the gods are real and that devotion is rewarded. The seventh god is not worshiped; the Seventh God is **The Rot**.

The Rot has no priesthood. It has no shamans or willing worshippers. It spreads like a parasite, corrupting flesh, beasts, forests, relics, and eventually faith itself. As followers of the Six Gods succumb, the Rot consumes them and their devotion, spreading its corruption upward into the gods they once served. Some gods resist it. Others are already changing.

The Rot spawns three types of creatures:

- **Aberration:** corrupted plants, mutated beasts, fungal horrors, eyestalks, flesh-vine hybrids, and entirely Rot-born creatures.
- **Construct:** corrupted ancient technology, machines, automatons, and animated artifacts.
- **Undead:** corpses, skeletons, revenants, fallen followers, and reanimated warriors.

At the center of the wasteland lies **the Valley**, the place where the Rot first appeared.

The chosen descend into The Valley. To survive, they must cooperate. They will gather Mana, acquire Equipment and Lore, confront corrupted creatures, and push deeper toward the source of the Rot.

The priesthood that ultimately destroys the Rot will claim victory for their deity, bringing that god immense power and elevating their faith above all.

## Setup

### 1. Choose Characters

Each player chooses a Character and takes:

- Their Character board
- Their Character-specific starting cards
  - 3 Artifact Cards
    - Weapon Card
    - Armor Card
    - Implement Card
  - 2 Lore Cards
- Action Tokens corresponding to the color of your Character

Distribute Gold, Mana, and Action Tokens based on the number of players:

| Players | Gold | Mana | Action Tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 | 8 | 3 |
| 2 | 5 | 10 | 3 |
| 1 (Solo) | 6 | 12 | 3 |

Mana is split evenly between your Artifact Attunement Pool and your Lore Attunement Pool.

**Solo Play**

When playing solo, use the standard rules with the following changes:

- You may not choose Kael or Taza as your character.
- The Arena is unavailable, you may not place an Action Token there. All other Locations function normally.

Complete your Character's Victory Condition and defeat The Rot to win the game.

### 2. Set Up Player Decks

Each player has two personal decks: an Artifact Deck and a Lore Deck.

Place your Character-specific starting Artifact cards in your Artifact Deck and your starting Lore cards in your Lore Deck. Shuffle each deck separately and place them face down beside your Character board.

Leave space beside each deck for its corresponding Artifact Discard Pile and Lore Discard Pile.

### 3. Set Up the Game Board

Place the game board in the center of the play area. The board contains ten Locations where players may place Action Tokens during the game. Two of these locations must be unlocked before you can go there.

Place 1 Gold on location "The Exchange."

**Faction**

Each player places 1 Mana Token at the bottom of the Faction Track at each of the core eight Locations.

Whenever you place an Action Token on a Location, move your Mana Token on that Location's Faction Track up 1 space. When your token reaches a space with a listed benefit, immediately gain that benefit.

### 4. Set Up the Artifact Deck

The Artifact Deck contains: Weapons, Armor, Implements, Rune Stones.

Shuffle the Artifact Deck and place it above the game board. Draw three cards and place them face up to form the Artifact Row.

The Artifact area is arranged: Discard Pile | Deck | Card 1 | Card 2 | Card 3

### 5. Set Up the Lore Deck

Shuffle the Lore Deck and place it below the game board. Draw three cards and place them face up to form the Lore Row.

The Lore area is arranged: Discard Pile | Deck | Card 1 | Card 2 | Card 3

### 6. Set Up the Rot Deck

Shuffle the Rot Deck and place it near the game board.

The Rot Deck represents the growing threat facing the Characters and ultimately determines when the final Rot Encounter occurs.

### 7. Determine First Player

The highest roll on 2d6 determines who goes first. The winner receives the First Player marker. Reroll ties.

The First Player marker rotates clockwise at the end of each round.

## Playing the Game

The game is played over a series of rounds.

Each player begins the round with their available Action Tokens.

Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, players take turns placing one Action Token and resolving the selected action.

Play continues until all players have used their Action Tokens, at which point you enter the Cleanup phase followed by a new round.

### Taking an Action

On your turn, place one of your available Action Tokens on a Location and, in any order:

1. Move your Mana Token on that Location's Faction Track up 1 space. Immediately gain the stated benefit. Most advances grant one time benefits, and some are ongoing.
2. Resolve that Location's action(s).
3. Activate any applicable attuned Runestones as desired.

A player may have only 1 of their Action Tokens on any Location. Multiple players may place Action Tokens on the same Location.

### 1. The Wellspring

**Wellspring Action:** Roll 2d6 to generate Mana.

Place that number of Mana tokens between your two Mana Pools:

- **Artifact Attunement Pool** — used to Attune Artifact cards
- **Lore Attunement Pool** — used to Attune Lore cards

**Wellspring Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location.
- 1 - Reroll any 1s or 2s on your "generate mana" roll this turn.
- 2 - You may choose to gather 7 mana instead of rolling every time you visit the Wellspring.
- 3 - When visiting The Wellspring, you may remove up to 2 Withering Tokens from either of your Mana Pools.

### 2. The Market

**Market Action:** When you take the Market action, you may purchase one of the three face-up cards in the Artifact Row. You may only purchase one Artifact card per visit.

To purchase a card:

1. Choose a face-up Artifact card.
2. Pay its Gold cost.
3. Take the card and place it in your Artifact Discard pile.
4. Immediately refill the empty position from the Artifact Deck.

**Market Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location.
- 1 - You may discard any or all cards in The Artifact Row and refill the empty position(s) from the Artifact Deck.
- 2 - You may move 1 Artifact card from The Rot Character Mat to The Artifact Row, replacing and discarding 1 Artifact card currently there.
- 3 - You may visit The Warriors Guild location if you have 3 faction at both The Market and The Artificer.

### 3. Library of Lore

**Library Action:** When you take the Library action, you may purchase one of the three face-up cards in the Lore Row. You may only purchase one Lore card per visit.

To purchase a card:

1. Choose a face-up Lore card.
2. Pay its Gold cost.
3. Take the card and place it in your Lore Discard pile.
4. Immediately refill the empty position from the Lore Deck.

**Library Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - You may discard any or all cards in The Lore Row and refill the empty position(s) from the Lore Deck.
- 2 - You may discard any or all cards in The Lore Row and refill the empty position(s) from the Lore Deck.
- 3 - You may visit The Scholars Guild location if you have 3 faction at both The Library and The Ancient Shrine.

### 4. The Artificer

This location allows Characters to Attune Artifact cards they have purchased, equipping them to their Character board. You may Attune as many Artifact cards as desired. To Attune an Artifact card:

1. Reveal the top card of your Artifact Deck. If your Artifact Deck is depleted, shuffle your Artifact discard pile to form a new deck.
2. You may pay its Mana cost from your Artifact Attunement Mana Pool. If you do not, place the card in your Artifact discard pile.
3. If you pay its Mana cost, place the Artifact on your Character board. If another Artifact occupies that slot, place the previous Artifact in your Artifact discard pile.
4. If you Attuned the Artifact, you may reveal the next card of your Artifact Deck and repeat this process. Otherwise, end the Attunement action.

**Cleansing:** When a Withering Token is placed in one of your Mana Pools, you must spend 1 Mana from that Pool to remove the Withering Token before you may spend Mana from that Pool for any other purpose.

**Artificer Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - You receive 4 Mana in your Artifact Attunement pool.
- 2 - You may look at the top two cards of your Artifact deck and place them back on top of your deck in any order.
- 3 - You may visit The Warriors Guild location if you have 3 faction at both The Artificer and the Market.

### 5. The Ancient Shrine

This location allows Characters to Attune Lore cards they have purchased, equipping them to their Character board. You may Attune as many Lore cards as desired. To Attune a Lore card:

1. Reveal the top card of your Lore Deck. If your Lore Deck is depleted, shuffle your Lore discard pile to form a new deck.
2. You may pay its Mana cost from your Lore Attunement Mana Pool. If you do not, place the card in your Lore discard pile.
3. If you pay its Mana cost, place the Lore card on your Character board. If another Lore card occupies that slot, place the previous Lore card in your Lore discard pile.
4. If you Attuned the Lore card, you may reveal the next card of your Lore Deck and repeat this process. Otherwise, end the Attunement action.

**Cleansing:** When a Withering Token is placed in one of your Mana Pools, you must spend 1 Mana from that Pool to remove the Withering Token before you may spend Mana from that Pool for any other purpose.

**Ancient Shrine Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - You receive 4 Mana in your Lore Attunement pool.
- 2 - Reveal the top 3 cards of The Rot deck. You may discard any of them, then return the remaining cards to the top of the deck in any order.
- 3 - You may visit The Scholars Guild location if you have 3 faction at both The Ancient Shrine and The Library.

### 6. The Exchange

The first Character each round to visit The Exchange takes all Gold on the Location.

You may perform any number of exchanges while visiting this location as follows:

1. Pay 1 gold to distribute 2 mana across your Artifact and Lore pools.
2. Select an Artifact Card from your discard pile and remove it from the game to receive half of its gold value rounded up, minimum of 1 gold.
3. Select a Lore Card from your discard pile and remove it from the game to receive half of its gold value rounded up, minimum of 1 gold.
4. Pay 3 mana to receive 1 gold.

**Exchange Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - You may increase your Faction by 1 at any location.
- 2 - You may increase your Faction by 1 at any location.
- 3 - Whenever you place an Action Token on The Exchange, add 1 Mana to each of your Mana Pools.

### 7. The Valley

Upon placing an Action Token on The Valley, remove 1 Withering Token from the board, then reveal the top card of the Rot Deck. There are two types of Rot cards: Combat and Scenario.

**Rot Combat Cards**

- **Use Lore:** Choose one of your attuned Lore cards and place it in your Lore discard pile to initiate the attack. Resolve any attack effects on that card. If you do not have a Lore card attuned, trigger the Loss condition.
- **Calculate Attack:** Add together: Roll 2d6 + the Attack value of the selected Lore card + the Attack values of all attuned Weapons, Armor, Implements, and Runestones.
- **Compare Attack:** Compare your total Attack against The Rot card's Attack value, plus the Attack values of all Weapons, Armor, and Implements on The Rot Character Mat.
- **Resolve the Attack:**
  - Your Attack ≥ The Rot's Attack: Resolve the Win condition on the Rot card. Place The Rot card in your player area.
  - Your Attack < The Rot's Attack: Equip The Rot with the highest-Gold-Cost Artifact from the Artifact Row. This Loss condition is universal — every Rot Combat card shares it, rather than printing its own.

**Rot Scenario Cards**

1. Read the Scenario.
2. Choose to Embrace The Rot or Resist The Rot.
3. Resolve the result associated with your choice.
4. Each time you Embrace The Rot, add 2 Withering Tokens to The Rot board, and place The Rot card in your player area.
5. Each time you Resist The Rot, place 1 Withering Token in each of your Attunement Pools.

**Valley Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - Place a withering token in each of your mana pools.
- 2 - Place a withering token in each of your mana pools.
- 3 - Whenever you place an Action token on The Valley, you may reveal the top card of The Rot deck. You may then choose to place it back on top of The Rot deck or discard it.

### 8. The Arena

The Arena allows Characters to challenge one another in combat for Gold, glory, and access to valuable cards.

**Entering the Arena**

1. Place an Action Token on the Arena.
2. Pay 1 Rot Card by placing it in the discard pile.

Combat is resolved at the start of the Cleanup Phase. At the start of the Cleanup Phase if any characters have an Action Token in the Arena, execute Arena Combat:

1. Each Character calculates their Attack: Roll 2d6 + the Attack values of all attuned Weapons, Armor, Implements, and Runestones. Lore cards do not factor into Arena combats.
2. The Character with the highest Attack wins.
3. If tied for the highest Attack, only the tied Characters reroll their 2d6 and recalculate their Attack. Continue until there is one winner.
4. If only one Character enters the Arena, they receive the Arena Reward without resolving combat.

**Arena Reward**

The winner reveals the top card of the Artifact Deck and the top card of the Lore Deck.

Choose one of the revealed cards and place it in your discard pile without paying its Gold cost.

Place the card you did not choose on the bottom of its deck.

**Arena Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - You may look at the top two cards of any deck and place the cards on the top or bottom as desired.
- 2 - Whenever you place an Action Token on The Arena, you may choose to pay 1 Gold entry instead of 1 Rot Card.
- 3 - You receive both Artifact and Lore cards as rewards on every victory in the Arena.

### 9. The Warriors Guild

The Warriors Guild allows the visitor to perform actions at both the Market and the Artificer as though they had placed their action token on both. You may purchase one card from The Market, and attune as many Artifact cards from your Artifact deck as you like.

**Warriors Guild Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - Place a card from the Artifact Row into your Artifact discard pile.
- 2 - Add 3 Mana to your Artifact Mana Pool.
- 3 - Recall one of your Action Tokens, you may immediately play it again.

### 10. The Scholars Guild

The Scholars Guild allows the visitor to perform actions at both the Library and the Ancient Shrine as though they had placed their action token on both. You may purchase one card from The Lore deck, and attune as many Lore cards from your Lore deck as you like.

**Scholars Guild Faction Track:**

- 0 - Starting location
- 1 - Place a card from the Lore Row into your Lore discard pile.
- 2 - Add 3 Mana to your Lore Mana Pool.
- 3 - Recall one of your Action Tokens, you may immediately play it again.

### Cleanup Phase

After all players have placed and resolved all of their Action Tokens, take the below "cleanup phase" actions:

**The Rot**

1. If the Rot Counter is 13 or higher, The Rot has consumed the players and wins the game.
2. Increase the Rot Counter by 1.
3. At Rot Counter 3, 6, and 9, place the highest Gold Cost Artifact card from the Market Row on The Rot Character Mat. If multiple Artifact cards are tied for the highest Gold Cost, choose one at random.
4. Remove 1 Withering Token per player from The Rot to increase the Rot Counter by 1. Repeat as able.
   - Example: In a 3-player game, remove 3 Withering Tokens and increase the Rot Counter by 1. Continue until fewer than 3 Withering Tokens remain.
5. Place 1 Withering Token per player on The Rot.

**The Game Board**

6. Resolve Arena combat if any Action Tokens are on The Arena.
7. Return all Action Tokens to their players.
8. Place 1 Gold on location "The Exchange."
9. Discard all face-up cards in the Artifact Row and Lore Row, then deal three new cards to each Row.

**Player Boards**

10. Remove any tokens on Character boards that indicate the use of a card.
11. Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, any player who has met their Character's Victory Condition may initiate The Final Battle. If no player does so, continue with the Cleanup Phase.
12. Pass the First Player marker clockwise.
13. Begin a new round.

## Ending the Game

If any player has met the Final Battle Condition on their Character Board, they may initiate The Final Battle and confront The Rot.

The first player to initiate The Final Battle receives a one-time boon from the gods. Before their first combat begins, that player may choose 1 Artifact or Lore card from their Deck or Discard Pile and Attune it without paying its Mana cost.

### The Final Battle

Starting with the initiating player and proceeding clockwise, each player who has not been defeated and has at least one Lore card Attuned takes a turn fighting The Rot.

For each combat:

1. Reveal cards from the Rot Deck until 2 Combat cards are revealed. Discard any Scenario cards revealed without effect. If the shared Rot Combat card supply (deck + discard) runs out of Combat cards entirely, The Rot is defeated.
2. Combine the Attack values of the 2 Combat cards to determine The Rot's base Attack.
3. Resolve combat as follows.

**Combat**

1. **Choose Lore:** Choose to use one or two Attuned Lore Cards and total the highest applicable Attack value of each, then discard them.
2. **Calculate Your Attack:** Add together:
   - 2d6
   - Your total Lore Attack
   - The Attack of all Attuned Weapons, Armor, Implements, and Rune Stones
3. **Calculate The Rot's Attack:** Add the Attack of both Combat cards and all Attack bonuses from cards on The Rot Character Mat.
4. **Resolve Combat:**

   | Combat Result  | Effect                                  |
   | -------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
   | Lose           | Remove 1 card from your Character Board |
   | Win by **0–4** | Remove **1 Rot Artifact**               |
   | Win by **5–9** | Remove **2 Rot Artifacts**              |
   | Win by **10+** | Remove **3 Rot Artifacts**              |

   Removed Rot Artifact cards come from The Rot Character Mat and are removed from the game. If you remove the last Artifact card, you defeat The Rot and win the game. If you have no cards remaining on your Character board after a loss, you are defeated and take no further turns in the Final Battle.
5. **Trophy:** Place both Rot Combat cards in your player area.
6. **Divine Reclamation:** Place your discarded Lore cards in your discard pile without shuffling. Choose 1 Lore card from your discard pile and Attune it without paying its cost.
7. **Press the Attack:** After winning combat, you may remove 1 Lore card from the game to immediately attack The Rot again, returning to Step 1. You may not remove your last Lore card this way. Otherwise, pass to the next combatant.
8. **Next Combatant:** The next player clockwise who has not been defeated and has at least 1 Lore card Attuned begins a new combat by revealing 2 Combat cards.

Continue until a player removes the last Artifact card from The Rot Character Mat or all players have been defeated.

If all players are defeated, The Rot wins the game.

### Final Ranking

After the final Rot Encounter, determine each player's final ranking:

- The player who deals the final blow by removing the last card from the Rot is the winner.
- Rank all remaining players by the total Gold value of their Attuned cards, from highest to lowest.
- If two or more players are tied, the tied player with the most cards in their Lore Deck ranks higher.
- If still tied, the tied players share that position.

## Game Elements

In Withering Worship, each player controls a Character seeking power while the influence of the Rot spreads across the world.

Players use Action Tokens to visit Locations, acquire Equipment and Rune Stones from the Market, discover Lore, generate Mana, and Attune cards to strengthen their Characters.

The game builds toward a final Rot Encounter. Players must develop their Characters in preparation for the threat that will ultimately determine the outcome of the game.

### Player Board

Each player has a Player Board used to manage their Character and Attuned cards.

The Player Board contains:

- **Artifact slots:** Weapon Slot, Implement Slot, Armor Slot, Rune 1 Slot, Rune 2 Slot, Rune 3 Slot.
- **Lore slots** for 3 Attuned Lore cards.
- **Mana Pools** used to store Mana for Attunement.
- Character-specific aspects as listed.

Only Attuned cards on your Player Board contribute their Attack values or provide their abilities unless a card specifically states otherwise.

### Player Characters

See `src/data/characters.ts` for the full structured list (Shar, Cira, Sylva, Taza, Legos, Kael).

### Artifact Cards

All Artifact cards come from the Market.

There are four types of Artifacts: Weapons, Armor, Implements, Rune Stones.

Artifacts must be Attuned at the appropriate Location before they can be placed on your Player Board and used.

Weapons, Armor, and Implements remain Attuned until replaced or removed. You may have only one Attuned Weapon, Armor, and Implement at a time.

You may have up to 3 Attuned Rune Stones.

All of your Attuned Artifacts contribute their Attack whenever you attack.

Rune Stones have abilities that require them to be discarded. A Rune Stone is not discarded simply because its Attack contributes to combat. You may choose to discard a Rune Stone to resolve its ability when permitted by the card.

If you discard a Rune Stone to use its ability during combat, its Attack still contributes to that combat if it was Attuned when the combat began.

### Lore Cards

Lore cards are acquired from the Lore deck and must be Attuned at the appropriate Location before they can be used.

You must have at least 1 Attuned Lore card to initiate an attack.

When attacking:

1. Select 1 Attuned Lore card.
2. Add its Attack value and any applicable combat effects to the attack.
3. After the combat resolves, place the selected Lore card in your Lore discard pile.

Only the selected Lore card contributes its Attack and effects to that combat. Other Attuned Lore cards remain Attuned.

### Player Attack

When you attack, calculate your total Attack:

**Attack = 2d6 + selected Lore Attack + Attack from all Attuned Artifacts**

Your Attuned Weapon, Armor, Implement, and all Attuned Rune Stones contribute their Attack values to every attack.

Artifacts are not discarded or exhausted when contributing their Attack unless a card or effect specifically instructs you to discard or remove them.

### The Valley Board

The Valley Board tracks the growing power of The Rot throughout the game.

It contains: the Rot Counter, area for Withering Tokens, 7 Artifact slots.

Artifacts placed on The Valley Board remain there until removed or replaced by a game effect.

All Artifacts on The Valley Board contribute their Attack values whenever a player battles The Rot.

Therefore: **The Rot's Attack = Rot Combat Attack + Attack from all Artifacts on The Rot Board**

The Rot can accumulate up to 7 Artifacts, making removal of its Artifacts increasingly important as the game progresses.

### Rot Cards

The Rot deck contains two types of cards: Combat and Scenario.

**Combat Cards**

Combat cards represent direct encounters with The Rot.

Each Combat card has an Attack value. When resolving combat, add the Combat card's Attack to the Attack of all Artifacts currently on The Rot Board.

Compare this total against the attacking Character's total Attack and resolve the combat as instructed by the encounter.

**Scenario Cards**

Scenario cards represent the influence and corruption of The Rot.

When a Scenario card is revealed, the player must choose whether to Embrace or Resist The Rot.

When you Embrace The Rot:

- Resolve the Embrace benefit printed on the Scenario card.
- Increase the Rot Counter by 1.
- Place the resolved Rot card in your player area.

When you Resist The Rot:

- Do not receive the card's Embrace benefit.
- Place 1 Withering Token in each of your Mana Pools.

Unless otherwise stated, Scenario cards do not initiate combat.
