# Withering Worship (digital prototype)

Digital prototype of the Withering Worship board game. React + TypeScript + Vite.

## Source material

- Rules: [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OumPNkl-xGU2d6HkwICOdILoHZDBYIJKn9jCRWgn1gQ/edit) — mirrored at [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md)
- Card data: [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1lexFGT82nryzwnrkpogQxA0oglJbc9rvTIQPDECGvF4/edit) — transcribed into `src/data/`

Both live in the "WW Game" Drive folder. If the doc or sheet changes, re-pull and update the copies here — this project does not read Drive live.

## Status

Not a full game yet, but the core turn loop is playable:

- All card/character data from the rulebook is typed and loaded (`src/types/game.ts`, `src/data/`).
- `src/engine/turnEngine.ts` implements round/turn state, Action Token placement (with the Warriors/Scholars Guild unlock conditions — multiple players may place on any Location, including the Wellspring), and the Cleanup Phase sequence.
- Faction benefits resolve the instant a Character's Faction track reaches that level (confirmed by design), and fall into two categories: **immediate** (a one-time choice/effect, e.g. Library's "cycle the Row" firing separately at Level 1 and again at Level 2) and **persistent** (an ability that stays active for the rest of the game, e.g. Wellspring Level 2's flat-7-Mana option being available on every future visit). Immediate choices queue onto `GameState.pendingFactionPerks` and must be resolved before that visit's main action.
- The Wellspring, The Market, Library of Lore, The Exchange, The Artificer, The Ancient Shrine, The Valley, and The Arena are implemented:
  - **Wellspring**: roll for Mana (Faction 1 reroll, Faction 2 flat-7 benefit), allocate it between the Artifact/Lore pools. Faction 3 ("remove 1 Withering Token from either Mana Pool") is real now too, once per-player Withering Tokens existed to remove.
  - **Market / Library of Lore**: buy from real shared, shuffled decks and 3-card Rows — Gold, per-player discard piles, and deck depletion all handled. Both Rows are fully discarded and redealt every Cleanup Phase (rulebook step 4), regardless of what was purchased that round. Faction milestones are real too: Market Faction 1 and Library Faction 1/2 offer a one-time "discard any/all of the Row and refill" choice, and Market Faction 2 offers a one-time "move 1 card from The Rot Character Mat (`state.rotArtifacts`) into the Artifact Row" choice — both fire the instant the track ticks up (confirmed by design: "these benefits happen concurrent with each Faction increase... one time benefits per faction increase"), not as a standing per-visit ability, and must be resolved (or declined) before that visit's purchase.
  - **Exchange**: first visitor each round claims the Gold sitting there (replenished by 1 each Cleanup Phase, accumulating if unvisited); all four exchanges are repeatable per visit — Gold-for-Mana, Mana-for-Gold, and Artifact- or Lore-discard-for-Gold. Faction 1/2/3 are each a one-time "increase your Faction by 1 at any Location" choice, fired the instant that tier is reached. Resolving it can itself trigger another Location's one-time Faction benefit (e.g. bumping Market to Faction 1 immediately offers that Row-refill choice) — these one-time Faction perks share a single queue (`GameState.pendingFactionPerks`, see `applyFactionMilestone`/`resolveExchangeFactionBump` in `turnEngine.ts`) so a cascade always resolves before the player can act again, even if it isn't the Location they're currently visiting.
  - **Artificer / Ancient Shrine**: each player has a real personal Artifact Deck (seeded from their character's Starter Set Weapon/Armor/Implement) and Lore Deck (seeded with 2 shared "Divine Presence" Starter Set cards), shuffled from their discard pile once depleted. Visiting reveals the top card; Attunement is if able *and* desired (confirmed by design) — the player may pay its Mana cost to Attune it (replacing whatever was in that Artifact slot, filling one of up to 3 Rune Stone slots, or filling/replacing one of 3 Lore slots — the player picks which, if all 3 are already Attuned) or send it to discard even if they can afford it. Attuning lets the player reveal again and repeat; **discarding ends the visit immediately** (confirmed by design — declining a card doesn't offer another reveal). Artificer Faction 1's "+2 Mana" is a real one-time bonus. Artificer Faction 2 is real too: look at the top 2 cards of your own Artifact deck once per visit and return them in whatever order you choose — no discard option, unlike the similar Ancient Shrine perk (`peekArtifactDeck`/`resolveArtifactPeek` in `turnEngine.ts`). The Ancient Shrine's Faction 1/2 perk is real too: peek the top 3 cards of the shared Rot Deck once per visit, choose which return to the top (in the player's chosen order), and discard the rest.
  - **The Valley**: removes 1 Withering Token from the shared Rot board, then reveals the top card of the shared Rot Deck. **Combat**: pick an Attuned Lore card to fight with (auto-Loss if none Attuned) — the correct Lore column applies by the Rot creature's type (`vsAberration`/`vsConstruct`/`vsUndead`, a clean 1:1 mapping confirmed by design), 2d6 + that value + all equipped Artifact/Rune Stone Attack is compared against the Rot card's Attack plus every Artifact ever equipped onto the Rot itself (`state.rotArtifacts`, up to 9, via "Equip The Rot with the highest-Gold-Cost Artifact from the Artifact Row"). **Scenario**: Embrace (Rot Counter +1, card to player area, resolve the bonus) or Resist (1 Withering Token in each Mana Pool). A shared effect resolver (`applyRotEffectText` in `turnEngine.ts`) covers every distinct Win/Loss/Embrace text in the card data — Gold/Mana gains (including player-chosen Mana splits), Rot Track advances, Withering Token placement/removal (including player-chosen pool), and "each opponent" effects. Visiting the Valley for the first or second time also triggers its Faction 1/2 downside for real (1 Withering Token in each of your own pools), and at Faction 3 the player may peek the top of The Rot Deck *before* the mandatory reveal and choose to keep it on top or discard it (`peekValleyRotCard`/`resolveValleyScry`/`skipValleyScry` in `turnEngine.ts`) — an ongoing per-visit ability, unlike the Ancient Shrine's similar once-per-visit peek. All 6 Scenario Embrace effects are real, including the 4 that need their own Row/deck picker UIs: **Corrupted Greed** (choose an Artifact Row card to discard, then the highest-Gold-Cost Row card auto-equips onto The Rot), **Tainted Trade** (offer a card from your own Artifact Deck or discard, trade it for a chosen Row card), **Forbidden Knowledge** (reveal the top 3 of the shared Lore Deck, keep 1 in your discard, drop the rest), and **Whispered Secrets** (pick one of 4 decks — your own Artifact/Lore or the shared Artifact/Lore — look at its top 5, and return them to the top in whatever order you choose; personal decks reshuffle their discard in if short, shared decks don't since there's no shared discard for them).
  - **The Arena**: entering costs 1 Rot card from the player's area (`EnginePlayer.rotCardsInPlayerArea`) — the player picks which *kind* (Combat or Scenario) to spend, since individual cards aren't tracked, just counts; unavailable with none, and in Solo Play per the rulebook. Combat is genuinely simultaneous — every Character with a token there fights together at the start of Cleanup Phase (not per-visit like every other Location), so `runCleanupPhase` resolves it as Cleanup step 1: 2d6 + all equipped Weapon/Armor/Implement/Rune Stone Attack (no Lore) per entrant, highest wins, ties reroll (2d6 only) only among the tied Characters until unique, and a single entrant wins automatically with no roll. The winner reveals the top card of the shared Artifact Deck and the top card of the shared Lore Deck and picks one for free (the other returns to the bottom of its deck) — a real choice, so Cleanup Phase pauses in a new `"arenaReward"` game phase until `resolveArenaReward` answers it, then finishes the rest of Cleanup (`resolveArenaCombatAndReward`/`finishCleanupPhase` in `turnEngine.ts`). Faction 2's "+1 Gold" is a real one-time bonus; Faction 3's "receive both cards on every win" is real and skips the pause (no choice needed); Faction 1's "look at the top two cards of any deck, place on top or bottom as desired" is real too, reusing the same 4-deck choice as Whispered Secrets (own/shared Artifact or Lore), with each card's top-vs-bottom destination chosen independently rather than just reordered (`peekArenaDeck`/`resolveArenaPeek`).
- **Cleansing** is enforced: "When a Withering Token is placed in one of your Mana Pools, you must spend 1 Mana from that Pool to remove the Withering Token before you may spend Mana from that Pool for any other purpose." Every Mana-spending action (Attuning at the Artificer/Ancient Shrine, the Exchange's Mana-for-Gold) throws if the relevant pool has any Withering Tokens; `cleansePool` is the prerequisite step, callable during the player's own turn (not tied to a specific Location, since the Withering Tokens and Mana Pools belong to the player, not a Location) — spend 1 Mana to remove 1 token, repeatable until the pool is clear. Gaining Mana (Wellspring, the Exchange's Gold-for-Mana) is unaffected, since only spending is restricted. Surfaced in the Artificer, Ancient Shrine, and Exchange panels as a Cleanse button whenever a relevant pool is tainted.
- **Victory Conditions and The Final Battle** are implemented. Each character's printed condition ("Have 3 Attuned Lore cards, at least 5 Rot cards, and [Guild access | 3 Faction at two named Locations]") is checked in `meetsVictoryCondition` (`turnEngine.ts`) — the Guild-access/Faction-pair half is parsed at runtime from `src/data/characters.ts`'s `victoryCondition` text (so that file stays the source of truth for *which* Locations matter per character), while the Lore/Rot-card thresholds are fixed constants shared by all 6 characters (bumped from 3 to 5 Rot cards by design request, prototype-only house rule not from the physical rulebook). Cleanup Phase step 6 checks it: starting with the First Player and going clockwise, each eligible Character (met their condition *and* has ≥1 Lore Attuned) is offered initiation in turn — declining moves to the next eligible Character; the whole thing pauses Cleanup Phase in a `"finalBattleOffer"` game phase until answered. Initiating starts `"finalBattle"`: reveal 2 Combat cards (discarding any Scenarios drawn along the way "without effect"), pick an Attuned Lore card to fight with (2d6 + Lore's vs-value for the *better* of the two revealed creature types + equipped gear Attack, vs. the combined Rot Attack + Rot Character Mat bonus) — winning removes 1 Artifact from The Rot Character Mat (empty it to win the game), losing permanently removes 1 card from the player's own board (no discard pile; an already-empty board after the Lore discard skips straight past this step). Either way, Divine Reclamation (freely Attune a Lore card from discard) is always offered next; after a win, Press the Attack lets the same Character sacrifice a Lore card (not their last) to fight again immediately instead of passing to the next Character. If a full clockwise scan finds nobody left able to fight, that's treated as a Rot win — not one of the rulebook's two named endings, but the same practical dead end. See `initiateFinalBattle`/`chooseFinalBattleLore`/`removeRotArtifact`/`removeOwnBoardCard`/`resolveDivineReclamation`/`resolvePressTheAttack`/`advanceToNextFinalBattleCombatant` in `turnEngine.ts`.
- **Character powers** are implemented for all 6 characters: Shar's Cleansing Fire (auto-rerolls one 1-or-2 die when attacking The Rot — always beneficial, so applied automatically like the Wellspring Faction 1 reroll), Cira's Foresight (the Valley's mandatory reveal draws 2 cards for her and lets her choose which to encounter, returning the other to the deck top — a new `pendingForesightChoice`, replacing the normal 1-card reveal only for her), Sylva's Nature's Bounty (-1 Gold at the Market on Primal/Corrupted Artifacts), Taza's Wield the Rot (+1 Attack vs. The Rot per Rot card in his player area), Legos's Grave Robber (an added "Attune directly from your Artifact discard pile" option at the Artificer, alongside the normal reveal-then-Attune loop), and Kael's Deep Waters (at the start of each of his turns — including a turn immediately following his own — he's offered a claim on one Artifact Row card: 1 Gold off for him, and other players are blocked from buying it at all until he does or the Row is redealt at Cleanup). Kael's offer is a new turn-boundary hook (`withDeepWatersOffer`, called from `createGame`, `completeTurn`, and the Cleanup Phase's round-advance) rather than tied to any Location visit, since nothing else in the engine happens outside of visiting one.
- **Backing out of a Location** is supported: a "← Back to Board" control appears whenever nothing further has happened since arriving (confirmed by design — this is a prototype UX convenience, not a rule from the physical game, where placing a token is the commitment). It fully undoes the visit — refunds the Action Token and reverts every automatic on-arrival effect (Faction gain, Valley's reveal, Exchange's gold claim, any queued Faction perk) — by rolling back to a snapshot of the exact state from just before the token was placed, rather than algebraically reversing each effect (`placeActionToken` captures it; `cancelLocationVisit`/`canCancelLocationVisit` in `turnEngine.ts`). Whether it's still valid is checked with a full-state JSON fingerprint comparison against the moment of arrival — the instant the player does anything else (rolls, reveals, buys, resolves a perk, trades...), the fingerprints diverge and the option disappears; no per-action bookkeeping needed to track "has something happened yet."
- **Final Ranking** is implemented for every way the game can end (a Final Battle win, a Final Battle with no Character left able to fight, or the Rot Counter reaching 13): `computeFinalRanking` in `turnEngine.ts` ranks players by total Gold value of their Attuned cards (weapon/armor/implement/rune stones/lore), breaking ties by Lore Deck size, and lets tied players share a rank (standard competition ranking — 1, 2, 2, 4). The Final Battle winner, if there is one, is always rank 1; a Rot win (Rot Counter hits 13, or nobody's left to fight) ranks everyone the same way with no winner marked. Computed once and stored on `GameState.finalRanking` the moment `phase` becomes `"gameOver"`; `FinalRankingPanel` in `GameBoard.tsx` renders it.
- `src/GameBoard.tsx` is a playable UI for 1-6 players covering all of the above.
- The shared Market's Artifact Deck is complete: 40 non-Starter Weapons/Armor/Implements plus all 36 Rune Stones (`runeStonesAsArtifacts` in `src/data/runeStones.ts` adapts the sheet's `RuneStoneCard` shape into `ArtifactCard`). A combined-slot card (Godslayer, "Weapon / Implement") makes the player choose which slot to occupy when Attuning, per the rulebook — `getArtifactSlots` in `turnEngine.ts` parses a card's slot(s), and `attuneArtifactCard` requires a `chosenSlot` argument whenever there's more than one.

- **Solo Play** is fully implemented: `createGame` starts a 1-player game with 3 Gold and 6 Mana split evenly (3 Artifact / 3 Lore) instead of the standard 1 Gold / 0 Mana, Kael and Taza are excluded from the Character picker (`Character.canPlaySolo`), and the Arena is unavailable (`isLocationAvailable` blocks it whenever `playerOrder.length === 1`).
- **Rune Stone discard abilities** are implemented for all 36 Rune Stones (rulebook: "You may choose to discard a Rune Stone to resolve its ability when permitted by the card"). Most are usable any time on the player's own turn, mirroring Cleansing's precedent — surfaced in a standing "Rune Stones" panel (`RuneStonesPanel` in `GameBoard.tsx`) that lists whatever's Attuned and opens a small per-stone form (`discardAbundance`, `discardCommunity`, `discardCulling`, etc. in `turnEngine.ts`); a handful that reveal hidden cards (Convergence, Discovery/Excavation/Prospecting/Study, Foresight, Insight, Seeking, Whispers) pause on a new `pendingRuneStoneChoice` instead, resolved by a matching picker (`RuneStoneChoicePanel`). 12 more are contextual to a specific Location/moment and threaded as optional params into that action's existing function rather than the standing panel: Barter (Exchange, inline button), Fortune and Resonance (a discounted alternative purchase/Attune button on Market/Library/Artificer/Ancient Shrine), Plenty (an alternative Wellspring roll button), Communion (a separate pay-from-either-pool Attune action at the Ancient Shrine), Defiance (an extra Withering-Token-removal button on Valley arrival), Premonition (piggybacks on the Valley's existing Faction-3 Scry pre-reveal pause — `placeActionToken` now opens that pause for a Premonition-holder too, even below Faction 3), Preservation (an alternate "Fight with X" button in Valley/Final Battle combat that discards the Rune Stone instead of the Lore card, which then stays Attuned), Vengeance (an inline form after a Valley/Final Battle loss), and Fracture (a "discard to reroll 1 die" pause between rolling and resolving Valley/Final Battle combat — `chooseValleyLore`/`chooseFinalBattleLore` only detour into this new `pendingValleyAttackRoll`/`pendingFinalBattleAttackRoll` pause when the combatant actually holds the stone; everyone else's combat still resolves in the original single call, so the common case has no added friction; `resolveValleyAttackRoll`/`resolveFinalBattleAttackRoll` finish it, with or without a reroll).

**Known gaps to fix before this is a full simulation:**
- **Bug**: `revealArtifactCard`/`revealLoreCard` (`turnEngine.ts`) silently no-op when a player's personal deck *and* discard pile are both empty — they log "has no Artifact/Lore cards left to reveal" but leave `pendingAction`/`pendingReveal` unchanged instead of ending the visit. This is reachable in normal play: a personal Lore deck starts with only 2 cards, and Attuning both (rather than discarding) never populates the discard pile to reshuffle from. In the UI this reads as "Reveal Next Card" doing nothing — no card, no error — until the player notices the log and clicks "Leave" instead; a caller driving the engine directly (as an automated playtest does) can spin forever re-calling reveal expecting a state change that never comes. Found while running an automated 3-player playtest simulation (2026-09-04). Fix: have both functions call `completeTurn` (or otherwise end the visit) when deck and discard are both empty, matching how a discarded reveal already ends the visit.
